Privacy & Consent Notice

AI-Based Classroom Attention Monitoring — LSOYS Platform

What This Tool Does

The Classroom Attention Monitor uses a classroom camera feed to estimate how many students appear visually engaged during a lesson — based on head orientation and eye openness detected by computer vision. The teacher sees a live class-wide attention percentage and a session report afterwards.

This is a teacher feedback tool, not a surveillance or disciplinary system. It is designed to help educators identify when a class may be losing engagement so they can adjust pacing or delivery — not to monitor, rank, or penalise individual students.

Accuracy Limitation — Important

The attention classifier uses geometric heuristics only (head yaw/pitch angles from camera + eye detection). It is not a validated measure of cognitive attention or learning. A student may look forward while mentally disengaged, or look sideways while fully engaged. Results should be treated as rough, directional signals — not as accurate data about any individual's mental state. Do not use this system as the sole basis for any academic, disciplinary, or welfare decision.

Data Collected & Minimisation

  • No raw video is stored. Camera frames are processed in memory and discarded after scoring.
  • No biometric data is stored. No face encodings, images, or unique identifiers of individuals are retained by the attention system.
  • Only aggregate, anonymised numeric metrics are persisted: class-wide attention percentage, timestamp, and face counts per 5-second interval.
  • In the default privacy mode (aggregate-only), not even anonymous per-slot data is shown in the UI.
  • Session reports contain only statistical summaries (avg %, dip timestamps). No individual data is included.

Bias & Fairness Notice

Computer-vision head-pose and eye-detection algorithms can perform unevenly across different skin tones, camera angles, lighting conditions, and facial features such as glasses, masks, or heavy eye shadow. This means classification accuracy is not uniform across all students. Teachers should be aware of this limitation and should not draw conclusions about individual students based on these estimates. We encourage testing with diverse faces and lighting conditions before deployment.

Consent & Governance

  • This tool should only be deployed with the explicit knowledge and consent of students, parents/guardians (where required), and institution administration.
  • Institutions must ensure compliance with applicable data protection law (India DPDP Act 2023, GDPR, FERPA, or equivalent) before deploying in a classroom.
  • This notice should be displayed to all users who will be monitored before a session begins.
  • Use of the system for individual disciplinary purposes is not permitted under this tool's intended scope.
